# Interface revision 0.4

Research checked 2026-10-11:
- Nielsen Norman Group, [complex application design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/complex-application-design/): prioritize relevant actions and reveal secondary detail progressively.
- GOV.UK Design System, [error summary](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/): keep errors visible and move focus to the explanation.

Applied: actionable risk cards above the overview, portfolio search/filter/sort, expandable product entry and advertising economics, persistent error summary, keyboard focus and skip link, clearer contrast, responsive wrapping, translated execution labels, and a judge-oriented route through the core demonstration.

The advertising allowance is a scenario calculation, not a safe CPC bid: additional costs and conversion probability still matter. No fabricated uplift, agent-learning claim, or active-account data is included in public screenshots.
